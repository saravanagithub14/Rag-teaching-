Marina Bean Cafe: Menu and Prices

Marina Bean Cafe is a small coffee shop near Kovalam beach, Thiruvananthapuram. It was opened in 2019 by Anjali Nair and her brother Rohit.

Hot drinks
Filter coffee is 40 rupees. Cappuccino is 120 rupees. Cardamom latte is 140 rupees. Our signature drink is the Coconut Cold Brew at 160 rupees, made with 18 hour cold brew and fresh tender coconut water. Masala chai is 50 rupees.

Food
The Banana Chip Toastie is 130 rupees and is the most ordered item. Egg puffs are 35 rupees each. The Malabar Veg Sandwich is 150 rupees. Every Tuesday we serve a special appam and stew combo for 220 rupees, only until 11 am.

Add-ons
Oat milk costs 30 rupees extra. An extra espresso shot costs 40 rupees. All drinks can be made sugar free on request at no extra charge.
