Marina Bean Cafe: About Us, Hours and Policies

Opening hours
We are open every day from 7 am to 10 pm, except on the first Monday of every month, when we close at 2 pm for deep cleaning. On Onam day we are closed completely.

Location
The cafe is on Lighthouse Road, a five minute walk from Kovalam beach. There is parking for about eight scooters and three cars. Free wifi is available. Ask at the counter for the password.

Ordering and payment
We accept UPI, cash and all major cards. We do not take credit on any order. Home delivery is available within 4 km through our own delivery boy, with a delivery fee of 30 rupees. Orders above 600 rupees get free delivery.

Refund policy
If a drink or dish is not right, tell us within 15 minutes and we will remake it for free. We do not give cash refunds for food that has already been eaten. For delivery orders, send a photo on WhatsApp within one hour if something arrives spilled or damaged and we will refund the item.

Events and bookings
Our upstairs hall seats 25 people and can be booked for birthdays and small meetups. The booking fee is 2000 rupees for up to 3 hours, adjustable against food and drinks. Book at least two days ahead by calling the cafe.

Pets
Well behaved dogs are welcome on the terrace, never inside. Our cafe cat is called Mocha and she is not for sale.
